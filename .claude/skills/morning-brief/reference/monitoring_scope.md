# Monitoring scope: what the brief tracks

Source: "Inception42 Daily Monitoring Overview" (Hawthorn Advisors, October
2026), section 2, with the "Daily Monitoring Procedure" source list. Roles
are as of October 2026; check a title before relying on it.

General rule: UAE and Abu Dhabi are the core priority; everything else is
tracked for what it means globally and for the Gulf. If unsure whether a
story is relevant, keep it as a candidate. Over-collect, then sort at
drafting.

## Categories (each is a section of the brief; see style_rules.md for placement)

**Power**: government AI policy moves (export controls, executive orders,
national AI strategies); US-China AI competition (chip restrictions, model
bans, talent flows); national security angles (military use, defense,
intelligence); frontier lab leadership changes, internal power struggles,
strategic pivots; sovereign AI initiatives from other nations (France,
Saudi Arabia, India, Kazakhstan, Malaysia).

**Regulation & Trust**: new AI laws and bills (US states, EU AI Act, UK,
China); court rulings involving AI companies (copyright, liability,
antitrust); safety incidents, red-teaming results, safety institute
findings, evaluations by NIST, UK AISI, CAISI; deepfake and misinformation
incidents with real-world impact; data privacy rulings or investigations
tied to AI training or deployment; cybersecurity, agent-control incidents,
opinion polls and surveys on AI.

**Enterprise & Investment**: funding rounds (flag even if unsure of size),
M&A involving AI companies; earnings where AI is a material driver
(Microsoft, Google, Meta, Nvidia, Oracle, Amazon); enterprise adoption case
studies and notable deployments; stock market moves clearly tied to AI
news; data center capex; macro and financial-stability angles (IMF, Bank
of England, SEC); AI-attributed job cuts.

**AI & Innovation**: product and model launches (any lab, any size),
notable open-source and open-weight releases; new capabilities and
benchmark results; research papers with significant industry
implications; agentic AI developments (frameworks, agent capabilities,
autonomy milestones); technical standards; robotics, world models, custom
AI chips.

**Alliances and Partnerships**: strategic partnerships between labs, cloud
providers or governments; joint ventures, especially cross-border;
infrastructure deals (compute agreements, data center partnerships); any
partnership involving Gulf entities with international players.

**Abu Dhabi & Gulf**: anything involving G42 and its ecosystem; new data
center announcements in the UAE or wider Gulf; Gulf sovereign wealth fund
AI investments (PIF, Mubadala, ADQ, QIA); UAE, Saudi and Qatar AI policy or
regulation; digital government (TAMM, ISO 42001, 6G); any article
mentioning Ashish Koshy or Ibrahim Al Jallaf directly (always flagged,
always its own bullet).

## Geographies

- Core priority: UAE and Abu Dhabi.
- Wider Gulf: Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman, Kuwait.
- Global: United States, China, European Union, France, UK, India, South Korea, Japan.
- National AI strategies also tracked: Singapore, Malaysia, Germany, Kazakhstan.

## Inputs the run reads

1. The two overnight Signal AI emails to Inception@hawthornadvisors.com
   (sender alerts@email.signal-ai.com, about 00:00 UTC): "Inception AI -
   corporate & regional news" (sections Inception mentions, G42 companies,
   OpenAI, Gulf/UAE region) and "Inception AI - AI & Themes".
2. The outlet sweep above, via built-in web search.
3. An explicit search for Inception42, Jais, Ashish Koshy and Ibrahim Al
   Jallaf mentions in the window (this replaces the manual Meltwater
   check; Meltwater has no usable connector).
4. Editor notes, if any, in `briefs/<date>/notes.md` (an upcoming
   Inception42 event, context not in the articles). The run never invents
   these.
