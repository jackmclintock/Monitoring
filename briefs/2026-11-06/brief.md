## Top Line
Political risk has moved from forecast to price across the AI trade. The Nasdaq 100 fell 6.4% on Thursday, erasing $2.5 trillion, as investors priced the risk that the Democratic sweep slows data center construction. The repricing is reaching contracts and credit, as Microsoft passed on more than doubling its Texas capacity with Nscale and Blue Owl expects its AI lending spreads to keep rising as new supply gets harder to add. Beijing is applying a brake of its own, holding about 600,000 Nvidia chips in Hong Kong rather than approving their import. OpenAI moved on two fronts, releasing GPT-6 Cyber to vetted security researchers with a “Critical” rating, the top tier of its safety framework, and meeting the US backlash with a community compact that commits it to pay for the power its future sites use. The link between energy and AI also ran through ADIPEC, which closed in Abu Dhabi with a record $67 billion in deals, led by ADNOC’s partnership with OpenAI and G42 through Inception42.

## Strategic Counter Narrative
UBS estimates that about 30% of the data center capacity the United States expects to add in 2028 could be held up by construction pauses, which moves the limit on frontier AI from chip supply to county permits and state grids. Demand for compute has not changed, so capacity that stalls in Texas, Ohio or Georgia will either cost more to deliver or be built in jurisdictions where power, land and public approval already line up. ADIPEC showed how closely Abu Dhabi now plans those pieces together, with an AI Zone a fifth larger than last year placing Microsoft, NVIDIA and IBM alongside the producers that supply their power, at an event hosted by the national energy company itself. The week’s leading agreement brought OpenAI’s frontier models into ADNOC through G42 and Inception42, applying that alignment inside the operations of one of the world’s largest energy companies. As ADIPEC closed on Thursday, energy and technology companies from around the world left Abu Dhabi with the same task of building compute and the power behind it in step, wherever they operate.
