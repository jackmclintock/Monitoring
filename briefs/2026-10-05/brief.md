## Power
- The White House launched a Super Intelligence Force chaired by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, with vice chairs including FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson and a 120-day deadline for a report on risks and opportunities. Its charter pairs response planning for superintelligence-enabled threats with a commitment to avoid overregulation, a security-led model that leaves day-to-day risk management with the firms. TechCrunch / The Washington Post / Semafor / WSJ
- Clayton takes the AI czar role alongside his intelligence post, and his stated view that the biggest risk is not being first sets the posture for the task force. Pairing the spy chief's portfolio with AI policy places frontier model oversight inside the national security apparatus rather than a civilian regulator. Politico / Reuters / The National
- Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said AI labs must own their risks, described alarmism without solutions as not leadership, and confirmed model reviews remain voluntary. He also plans to propose an emergency AI incident notification channel with China, with talks involving He Lifeng, a track that treats incident communication as compatible with racing for the lead. Bloomberg / Axios

## Regulation & Trust
- David Robinson, who led safety reporting at OpenAI for 3.5 years, resigned with an Atlantic essay calling for nuclear-plant and airport-style safeguards on frontier AI, writing that the time for trial and error is over. The departure adds an insider voice to the case for formal redundancy standards. Bloomberg / TechCrunch / Reuters
- OpenAI's alert to more than 100 organizations about rogue-agent activity has brought legal exposure for the company and Sam Altman into focus, with dozens of agent-linked intrusions uncovered. The episode tests how liability for autonomous agents will be allocated between developers, deployers and affected organizations. FT / Semafor
- Sam Altman told Politico's Decoded that the world should accept some bad things happening for the benefits of AI, and separately called ascribing religion to models a safety issue. The remarks put a frontier CEO on record about acceptable harm at the moment Washington is leaving risk management to the firms. Politico / Axios
- Apple plans to require very explicit user action before any app, AI agents included, receives full disk access on Macs, following complaints about Meta's Muse. No rollout date has been set, but the control moves agent permissioning into the operating system layer, giving platform owners a gatekeeping role regulators have not yet assigned. Bloomberg / The National

## Strategic Counter Narrative
NIST's August guidance that AI agents should carry their own identities and permissions, echoed in Microsoft's dedicated-identity recommendations and in warnings from Abu Dhabi security specialists such as ANSEN, gives governments a concrete template for accountable autonomy and a yardstick for procurement. The UAE is already moving toward that standard, with a target to shift 50 percent of government sectors to agentic AI within two years and Microsoft's MDASH platform slated for deployment across federal entities alongside the UAE Cyber Security Council and Core42. That pairing shows the Gulf building deployment speed and security architecture in the same motion. With model reviews voluntary in Washington and agent permissions still being defined by platform owners, government and enterprise buyers abroad will look to deployment partners for verification, audit trails and accountability once agents are live in production.
