## Top Line
Outside verification is becoming a condition of selling frontier AI, and the labs are setting its terms before regulators do. Anthropic gave six METR researchers employee-level access with no right to block publication, and OpenAI has pledged the same arrangement, so outside inspection is turning into a norm that competitors must match. Washington is moving in parallel: the White House Super Intelligence Force has 120 days to report on cybersecurity, biosecurity and model control, yet how any of it would be enforced remains undefined, with export controls floated as one option. Perplexity’s release of its agent sandbox as open source adds a yardstick, with no escapes in 118 attempts across nine frontier models, which gives buyers a concrete number to compare. Distribution is still accelerating, with Meta and Apple putting an open-weight model on Macs and Abu Dhabi preparing to license TAMM to other governments, so buyers will increasingly ask what was inspected before they ask what the system can do.

## Strategic Counter Narrative
Anthropic’s agreement with the evaluator METR, announced yesterday, spells out the terms of outside verification: six researchers work from company laptops with internal tools, Anthropic may redact only narrow categories such as customer data, privileged material and model architecture, and METR can say publicly whether it considers each redaction justified. Terms that precise give enterprise and government buyers a reference point for what to ask any provider of agentic systems, from incident reporting to containment testing. In the Gulf, that question arrives with deployments already running, since TAMM serves millions of residents and the UAE has been receiving Nvidia chips since May, so the services and the infrastructure are in place while verification standards are still being written. Governments that adopt AI at this pace will want providers able to show how their systems are monitored, who can inspect them and what happens when something fails. Inception42’s Jais 3 release with MBZUAI’s Institute of Foundation Models, announced yesterday, adds Arabic-native speech, writing and agent capabilities to that landscape. Discussions at AI Everything in Abu Dhabi, spanning data readiness, agentic AI and human oversight, show how widely these questions are shared across sectors and countries.
