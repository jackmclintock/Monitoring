## Top Line
Frontier AI is now financed like infrastructure, through lease vehicles, record equity raises and bond issuance, and the price of that shift is a thinner margin for error across the sector. Anthropic’s formal filing for a Nasdaq IPO sets the tone, revealing soaring revenue and a governance model that leaves public shareholders with little say, alongside a same-day $63 billion Broadcom chip deal under which lenders buy the hardware and lease it to the lab. Meta’s $95 billion stock offering and SpaceX’s $40 billion GPU lease with Apollo confirm that even the largest cash generators can no longer self-fund the buildout, while US high-grade bond sales reached an all-time annual record. Microsoft’s buyout offer to roughly 60,000 employees shows the other side of the ledger, with workforce reduction offsetting the cost of the AI push. In the Gulf, G42’s Peng Xiao pitched the UAE as an exporter of intelligence at the Dubai AI Festival, a claim backed by new data ranking the Emirates among the cheapest of 62 markets to build data centers.

## Strategic Counter Narrative
Anthropic’s IPO filing reveals $417 billion in future commitments for computing capacity and data center leases, much of it for facilities not yet built, commitments that assume its revenue keeps growing at the current pace. Under the $63 billion Broadcom deal, the chipmaker also agreed to cover part of the lenders’ losses if the chips lose value, so Broadcom, the banks and Anthropic are now tied to the same bet on AI demand. The UAE enters this cycle on a different footing, with infrastructure backed by sovereign capital and domestic energy, and, as G42’s Peng Xiao noted, American partners already coming to the country to secure compute. At the Dubai AI Festival, Minister of State for AI Omar Al Olama warned that organizations relying only on public models risk displacement, placing the real advantage in how well AI is used inside their own operations. The festival’s second day will show how quickly governments and companies from the region and beyond are turning AI investment into measurable results.
