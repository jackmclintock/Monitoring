## Strategic Counter Narrative
In September, the heads of Anthropic, OpenAI and SpaceX publicly agreed that frontier AI should slow down. Six weeks later, all of their companies have released more capable models, and the White House is still refusing to publish the framework it uses to test those models before release. Voluntary promises and undisclosed standards have not produced real oversight. In the meantime, control is coming from rules that companies apply themselves, as JPMorgan and Amazon now decide which AI agents can access their customers' accounts. The UAE is writing those rules down, with Dubai Chambers scoring companies on human oversight, cybersecurity and measurable results before rewarding their use of agentic AI. Abu Dhabi's new agreements with OpenAI apply the same logic, with every AI-assisted government assessment traceable to its governing rule and final decisions left to officials. Inception42 will bring that model to ADNOC and Mubadala, on inference kept inside the UAE. At ADIPEC this week, energy operators and technology companies from around the world will face that question directly: how far AI can be trusted inside the systems that keep power and fuel flowing.
